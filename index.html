Future Humanoid Language Teachers

C1 Interactive Activity · Work at your own pace · Instant feedback · No teacher required
Goal: Deepen understanding of a complex speculative text on AI, robotics and language learning.

1. Read the Text Carefully

Post 2035, offplanet AI will lead to further improvements in efficiency for humanoid robots teaching a second language.

For example, larger-scale, more frequent fine-tuning enabled by abundant orbital solar power could produce significantly stronger multimodal vision-language-action models. These models would allow a humanoid robot to interpret a learner's subtle facial microexpressions, posture shifts, and gaze patterns with higher accuracy across many sessions.

As a result, the robot could detect rising speaking anxiety or momentary confusion earlier and respond in real time by adjusting its own facial expression, gesture intensity, speech rate, or scaffolding level more precisely and naturally. This would improve the quality of affective support and reduce the affective filter during one-to-one conversation practice beyond what models trained under terrestrial energy and cooling constraints can typically achieve.

4. Critical Thinking & Inference

A. Why might “offplanet” (orbital) computing offer advantages that terrestrial systems currently lack?

Possible points: energy abundance, cooling, continuous power, scale of computation…

B. How could real-time adjustment of scaffolding and affective support change the experience of speaking practice compared with a human teacher or current apps?

Consider consistency, lack of judgement, precision of detection, personalisation over many sessions…
